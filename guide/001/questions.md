# Questions

Hunt on your own and check answers as you go. Your score is saved in this browser only. Each question has a hint, the answer, and a link to the walkthrough step that solves it.

Answers are not case sensitive unless the question says so. For questions with several values, separate them with commas, in any order.

!!! tip "Running a CTF?"
    The same questions export to CTFd with `scripts/export_ctfd.py`. See [the exporter docs](https://github.com/zaneGittins/bifract-labs/blob/main/docs/ctfd-export.md).
