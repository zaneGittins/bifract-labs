# Questions

Hunt on your own and check your answers as you go. Your score is only saved in this browser. Each question has a hint, the answer, and a link to the part of the walkthrough that covers it.

Answers aren't case sensitive unless the question says so. When a question asks for several values, separate them with commas, in any order.

!!! tip "Running a CTF?"
    You can export these questions to CTFd with `scripts/export_ctfd.py`. See the [exporter docs](https://github.com/zaneGittins/bifract-labs/blob/main/docs/ctfd-export.md).
