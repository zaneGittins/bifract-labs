# Bifract Labs

Realistic security datasets for CTFs, analyst training, and detection engineering. Each lab is a full environment's logs with an intrusion inside, plus a guided investigation you can follow in [Bifract](https://github.com/zaneGittins/bifract), a self-hosted, open source SIEM built on ClickHouse.

## Labs

| Lab | Scenario | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| [001](001/index.md) | Meridian workstation intrusion | ClickFix, renamed binaries, remote access tools, password spraying, lateral movement |

Datasets are attached to [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/zaneGittins/bifract-labs/releases).

!!! warning "Do not contact indicators"
    Attacker domains in these datasets are fictional, and attacker IP addresses come from documentation ranges. Do not resolve, browse to, or otherwise contact any indicator from a dataset. Fictional domains were unregistered when published, but someone could register them later.
